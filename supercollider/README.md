# ASH (SuperCollider)

A 3-minute generative piece: electroacoustic, cinematic, tape-saturated, with
a death-industrial core. Synthesised in SuperCollider (with sc3-plugins for
the JPverb reverb) and rendered offline to WAV.

| Time | Section |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Rust.** Drone out of tape hiss, bowed sheet metal, scrapes, detritus, a ghost voice |
| 0:40 | **Machine.** Slow limping industrial hits, tritone drone, struck and bouncing metal, grains |
| 1:20 | **Furnace.** Sub impact, a 50-second noise wall, bent bowed metal, granular storm, hard cut |
| 2:10 | **Ash.** Reverb tail, drone, distant bells; the tape wobbles, saturates and fades |

Render: `./render.sh [seed]`, or open `ash.scd` in the SuperCollider IDE and
run it. A different seed gives a different take on the same form.
